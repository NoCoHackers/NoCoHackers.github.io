# NoCo Hackers

A community of cybersecurity professionals and enthusiasts in Northern Colorado dedicated to learning, sharing knowledge, and having fun with all things security.

## About

We meet on the **third Thursday of every month** at 6:00 PM MST at [Purpose Brewing](https://maps.app.goo.gl/x7jx5fEEr9748dgr5) in Fort Collins, CO.

Whether you're a seasoned pentester, a curious beginner, or somewhere in between, you're welcome here. Our meetups feature hands-on workshops, CTF challenges, tool demos, and technical talks.
