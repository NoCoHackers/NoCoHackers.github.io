NoCo Hackers is a community of cybersecurity enthusiasts and professionals in Northern Colorado. We're passionate about security research, ethical hacking, and building a welcoming space for anyone interested in the field.

# What We Do

- **Capture The Flag (CTF) competitions** — Practice offensive and defensive security skills
- **Security tool demonstrations** — Hands-on learning with the latest tools
- **Networking and career development** — Connect with local security professionals
- **Technical talks and presentations** — Share knowledge and learn from peers
